**What we process**
- Voice: speech is captured only after you tap the microphone or the notification's Listen button, and is converted to text by your device's speech recognition service (provided by Google/your OEM, under their policies).
- Text you send is transmitted over HTTPS to our server, which forwards it to an AI provider to generate a reply. We do not store it on our server [edit if you do].
- Conversation history is stored only on your device, only if you opt in. You can delete it anytime in Settings.
- Contacts are read only when you ask to call/text by name, and are not uploaded.

**What we don't do**
No ads, no selling data, no continuous or hidden recording, no bypassing of Android security.
